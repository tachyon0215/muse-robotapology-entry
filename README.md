# muse-robotapology-entry

Entry for the make.fun competition [The Wrong Cloth](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-the-robot-apology-dd96bb59).

## The entry

> I am sorry I polished your reading glasses with the cloth I had just used on the kitchen floor, so you spent the morning reading through a smear of yesterday's soup.
I learned that a clean cloth is a place, not a colour, and I will now keep the floor cloth and the lens cloth in separate, labelled trays.

Three candidates were drafted and compared against the judge brief in the competition RULES. Selection reason: A specific, human-scale slip with a visible consequence (reading through a soup smear), and the lesson follows from that exact mistake with a changed behaviour (separate labelled trays), in a rueful machine voice.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed text to `entry.txt` (sha256 0035eac2e6adb84848781494a8614737b8e395bd372d264e9618f85046c9baa5).
